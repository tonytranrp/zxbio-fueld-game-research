# Biofuel Feedstocks & Crops: What Biofuels Are Made From

## Feedstock Categories

Biofuels can be made from an enormous variety of biological materials,
grouped by generation and type.

---

## First-Generation Feedstocks (Food Crops)

### For Ethanol

| Crop | Where Used | Yield per Acre | Process |
|------|-----------|----------------|---------|
| Corn (maize) | US, China, EU | 370-430 gal ethanol/acre | Starch → sugar → ferment |
| Sugarcane | Brazil, India | ~590 gal ethanol/acre | Direct sugar → ferment |
| Wheat | EU, Canada | ~270 gal ethanol/acre | Starch → sugar → ferment |
| Sugar beets | EU | ~550 gal ethanol/acre | Direct sugar → ferment |
| Sweet sorghum | India, Africa | ~250-400 gal/acre | Direct sugar → ferment |

**Global ethanol feedstock breakdown (2021):**
- 62% Maize (corn)
- 31% Sugarcane
- 1% Other wastes and residues

### For Biodiesel

| Crop/Oil | Where Used | Oil Yield (gal/acre) | Notes |
|----------|-----------|---------------------|-------|
| Soybean oil | US, Argentina, Brazil | ~48 gal/acre | Most common in US |
| Palm oil | Indonesia, Malaysia | ~635 gal/acre | Highest-yielding oil crop, controversial |
| Rapeseed (canola) | EU, Canada | ~127 gal/acre | Primary EU biodiesel feedstock |
| Sunflower oil | EU, Russia | ~98 gal/acre | — |
| Jatropha | India, Africa | ~200 gal/acre | Grows on marginal land, toxic seeds |
| Coconut oil | Philippines | ~287 gal/acre | — |

**Global biodiesel feedstock breakdown (2021):**
- 30% Palm oil
- 22% Soybean oil
- 14% Used cooking oil
- 12% Rapeseed oil
- 9% Animal fats

## Second-Generation Feedstocks (Non-Food, "Advanced")

### Agricultural Residues

- **Corn stover** — leaves, stalks, cobs left after corn harvest
- **Wheat/rice straw** — stems left after grain harvest
- **Sugarcane bagasse** — fibrous residue after crushing sugarcane for juice
- **Nut shells, fruit pits** — almond shells, olive pits, etc.

### Forestry Residues

- Wood chips, sawdust, bark
- Forest thinning (fire prevention byproduct)
- Black liquor (paper mill byproduct)

### Dedicated Energy Crops (Grown on Marginal Land)

| Crop | Type | Yield (dry tons/acre) | Notes |
|------|------|----------------------|-------|
| Switchgrass | Perennial C4 grass | 5–10 | Native to North America, deep roots |
| Miscanthus | Perennial C4 grass | 10–20 | Asian origin, very high yields |
| Poplar (hybrid) | Fast-growing tree | 5–8 | Short rotation coppice |
| Willow | Fast-growing tree | 4–7 | Short rotation coppice |
| Industrial hemp | Annual | 3–8 | Multi-use: fiber, oil, biomass |
| Arundo donax (giant reed) | Perennial grass | 15–25 | Invasive concerns, very high yield |

These crops can restore degraded land, sequester carbon in deep roots, and
reduce erosion — providing ecosystem services beyond fuel production.

### Waste Streams

- **Used cooking oil (UCO) / yellow grease** — from restaurants, food processing
- **Municipal solid waste (MSW)** — organic fraction of trash
- **Food waste** — grocery stores, restaurants, households
- **Animal fats / tallow** — slaughterhouse byproducts
- **Sewage sludge** — wastewater treatment plants

## Third-Generation Feedstocks (Algae & Aquatic)

### Microalgae

Tiny photosynthetic organisms that produce oil (lipids) internally.

| Species | Lipid Content | Notes |
|---------|--------------|-------|
| *Chlorella* | 28-32% | High productivity, well-studied |
| *Botryococcus braunii* | 25-75% | Produces hydrocarbons directly |
| *Nannochloropsis* | 31-68% | Marine species, high EPA (omega-3) |
| *Schizochytrium* | 50-77% | High DHA, used for nutraceuticals too |

### Cultivation Methods

| Method | Cost | Yield | Control | Water Loss |
|--------|------|-------|---------|------------|
| Open raceway ponds | Low | Lower | Minimal | High evaporation |
| Closed photobioreactors | High | Higher | Precise | Low |

### Macroalgae (Seaweed)

- **Kelp, seaweed farms** — grow in ocean, no land/freshwater needed
- Can produce biogas, ethanol, or bio-crude via HTL
- Kelp can grow 1-2 feet per day in ideal conditions

### Yield Comparison

| Feedstock | Oil Yield (gal/acre/year) |
|-----------|--------------------------|
| Soybean | ~48 |
| Rapeseed | ~127 |
| Jatropha | ~200 |
| Palm oil | ~635 |
| Algae (theoretical) | ~5,000–10,000 |
| Algae (lab-optimized) | Up to 15,000 |

## Fourth-Generation Feedstocks (Engineered)

- **Genetically modified algae** — CRISPR-edited for higher lipid production,
  faster growth, salt tolerance
- **Engineered bacteria (E. coli)** — modified to produce butanol, isobutanol,
  or even diesel-like hydrocarbons directly from sugars
- **Engineered yeast** — produce ethanol more efficiently, or produce
  advanced alcohols like isobutanol
- **Synthetic biology consortia** — multiple engineered organisms working
  together in a bioreactor

## Input Requirements (Game-Relevant Numbers)

### What It Takes to Grow Feedstock

| Input | Corn (per acre) | Soybeans (per acre) | Sugarcane | Switchgrass |
|-------|----------------|--------------------|-----------|-------------|
| Nitrogen fertilizer | 140-180 lbs | 0-20 lbs (legume) | 80-120 lbs | 50-80 lbs |
| Phosphorus (P₂O₅) | 60-80 lbs | 40-60 lbs | 40-60 lbs | 10-20 lbs |
| Potassium (K₂O) | 50-70 lbs | 60-80 lbs | 80-120 lbs | Minimal |
| Water (irrigated) | 18-24 inches | 16-22 inches | 40-60 inches | Minimal (rain-fed) |
| Pesticides | High | Moderate | Moderate | Very low (perennial) |
| Diesel/tractor fuel | ~6 gal/acre | ~5 gal/acre | Variable | ~3 gal/acre |

### Environmental Trade-offs

| Feedstock | Land Use | Water Use | Fertilizer Runoff | Biodiversity | Carbon Storage |
|-----------|----------|-----------|-------------------|-------------|----------------|
| Corn | High | High (irrigated) | High (Gulf dead zone) | Low (monoculture) | Low |
| Soybeans | High | Moderate-High | Moderate | Low | Low |
| Sugarcane | Moderate | High | Moderate | Low | Moderate |
| Palm oil | Moderate | High | Moderate | Very Low (deforestation) | Low |
| Switchgrass | Low (marginal land) | Low (rain-fed) | Very Low | High (perennial) | High (deep roots) |
| Miscanthus | Low | Low | Very Low | Moderate-High | High |
| Algae | Very Low | Low (salt/brackish) | None | N/A | N/A (absorbs CO₂) |
| Used cooking oil | None (waste) | None | None | N/A | N/A |
