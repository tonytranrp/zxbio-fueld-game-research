# Human Eye Research — Parent Question Bank (50 questions)

Deep-research questions generated before delegation. Each domain agent receives its subset plus the instruction to generate 40 more of its own; agents must answer or explicitly dispose of every question in their final file.

## Optics & resolution (agent 1)
1. What is the eye's total optical MTF — diffraction, aberrations, scatter combined — and at what spatial frequency does it actually fall to zero?
2. Why does acuity peak at a ~2–3 mm pupil and fall off for both smaller and larger pupils?
3. What are the exact aberration budget numbers (defocus, astigmatism, longitudinal chromatic aberration ~2 D across the visible spectrum, higher-order Zernike terms) and how do they trade against diffraction?
4. What is the cone Nyquist limit (60–73 cyc/deg from 2–2.5 µm foveal cone spacing) and what happens when optics out-resolve it (aliasing)?
5. How do Stiles–Crawford effect, pupil apodization, and iris color affect the effective pupil?
6. What is the measured depth of focus (±0.25–0.3 D typically quoted) and how does it scale with pupil size?
7. How does accommodation work mechanically (ciliary muscle, zonules, lens stiffening with age) and what is the amplitude curve from childhood to presbyopia?
8. What is the accommodative lag/lead and how does it drive myopia development?
9. How does longitudinal chromatic aberration interact with accommodation cues?
10. How does the tear film (3-layer, ~3 µm) shape the optical surface and what happens when it breaks up (dry-eye blur, refractive shifts between blinks)?

## Phototransduction & adaptation (agent 2)
11. What is the phototransduction cascade's actual gain (single photon → ~10^5–10^6 cGMP molecules hydrolyzed?) and speed (rod response peaks ~100–200 ms)?
12. What are the measured dark-adaptation timescales — rod/cone plateau split, the Kohlrausch kink, full dark adaptation ~30–40 min?
13. How do rod and cone pathways differ in temporal response (rods slower and more persistent, cones faster) and what does that do to motion perception in dim light?
14. What is the Pulfrich effect's mechanism and magnitude (interocular delay ~ms per log unit luminance difference)?
15. How does the pupillary light reflex work — latency, amplitude, the constriction/dilation asymmetry (dilation is slower)?
16. What is the full scotopic→photopic luminance range the eye spans (~10–12 log units) and where are the adaptation breakpoints?
16b. What is contrast sensitivity as a function of spatial frequency (the contrast sensitivity function), how does it differ between photopic and scotopic/mesopic vision, and where is its peak (~3–8 cyc/deg)?
17. What exactly is "bloom" perceptually — stray light, scatter, and the veiling luminance formula?
18. How does glare (disability vs. discomfort) degrade resolution with angle from a bright source?
19. What are the actual response latencies of the retina → LGN → V1 (~30–50 ms) and how do luminance and eccentricity shift them?
20. How does the retinal image fade without eye movements — and why does the stabilized-image paradigm matter?

## Eye movements (agent 3)
21. What are the complete kinematics of saccades (main sequence: duration = a + b·amplitude, peak velocity saturation ~700–900°/s, skewing) with fitted constants from the original sources?
22. Why can't saccades be corrected mid-flight (the 150–200 ms corrective-latency argument)?
23. What is saccadic suppression exactly (threshold elevation ~0.5–1 log unit, time course ±50 ms) and what is its mechanism (corollary discharge vs. retinal smear)?
24. What is the attentional "pre-saccadic shift" — does attention move to the saccade target before the eye does, and by how much (~50–100 ms)?
25. What are microsaccades (amplitude < 1°, ~1–2 Hz), why do they exist, and do they prevent Troxler fading?
26. What happens perceptually during fixation — drift, microsaccades, and the resulting retinal image motion (~tens of arcmin/s)?
27. What is Troxler fading and why does peripheral static images fade (cortical adaptation in the absence of retinal image motion)?
28. What happens during a very fast glance to the side — the complete timeline (pre-saccadic attention shift → saccade ~50 ms → suppression → post-saccadic re-fixation), and why is the world perceived as stable despite the 700°/s retinal smear?
28b. Why does a clock sometimes appear to stop after a saccade (chronostasis) and what does that say about time perception during eye movements?
29. What is optokinetic nystagmus (OKN) — the slow-phase/slow-fast-phase alternation, its optokinetic fusion limits, and why does it exist?
30. What is vestibular nystagmus (post-rotatory) — why does the world appear to move after spinning stops, and how long does it last (canal time constant, velocity storage)?
31. What is the lifespan of a visual percept that enters during a saccade — can a stimulus be seen if flashed only during saccadic suppression?
32. What are the fixational eye movement statistics during real tasks (reading scanpaths, scene viewing, search) vs. laboratory fixation?
33. How does smooth pursuit start up (pursuit initiation latency ~100–150 ms) and why can't it be initiated without a moving target?
34. What happens when pursuit fails and the target slips — catch-up saccades and their triggering rule?

## Fixation, attention & perception (agent 4)
34b. When you lock onto an object, what measurably happens at the surrounding locations — perceptual enhancement at the target vs. suppression around it (surround suppression)? With measured spatial extents and time courses?
35. What is the attentional spotlight/zoom-lens — does it truly "move" or does it "shrink"?
36. What is inhibition of return (time course ~200 ms onward, spatial extent) and why does it exist (foraging)?
37. What is the attentional blink (RAP stream, ~200–500 ms window) and what does it imply about conscious processing rate?
38. What is change blindness and why is it so extreme (details of the flicker/mud-splash paradigms, inattentional blindness rates)?
39. What is the Eriksen flanker effect — how far does interference extend (a few degrees?) and how fast is it resolved?
40. What is the binding problem and how does attention solve it (feature integration theory)?
41. What is visual working memory capacity (~3–4 items) and how does fixation interact with it (chunks, saccade-target VWM)?
42. How do feature-based and space-based attention interact (guided search, danger of circular reasoning)?
43. What is the "just noticing" threshold — how much does something have to change for detection without fixation on it?
44. What is motion-induced blindness (MIB) and how does it differ from Troxler fading?
45. How does depth ordering and figure-ground segregation happen pre-attentively (surface completion, border ownership)?
46. What are aftereffects (motion aftereffect/waterfall illusion, tilt, size) — mechanism (adaptation), magnitude, and decay?
47. What is the flash-lag effect and what does it imply about the visual system's temporal processing (postdiction)?
48. How does crowding work in the periphery (Bouma's law: critical spacing ~0.5 × eccentricity) — why can't you recognize a face at 10° even though single letters are acuity-limited?
49. How does perceived direction of gaze/attention of others affect our own attention (gaze cueing)?
50. How does emotional salience (threat superiority, face detection) modulate what gets fixated first?
