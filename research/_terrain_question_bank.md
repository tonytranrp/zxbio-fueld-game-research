# Earth Terrain & Geomorphology — Parent Question Bank (Task 4)

Written BEFORE subagent delegation, per the established research pipeline. 60 questions across the
seven delegation domains. Each subagent additionally generates 40 questions of its own (answered or
explicitly dispositioned in its appendix). Numeric anchors distributed to agents are pre-computed
or pre-sourced by the parent and must be independently verified — disagreements are reported, not
silently copied.

## A. Tectonics, mountain building & planetary structure (Agent 1)

1. Why do mountain ranges form in long linear belts instead of isolated random spots?
2. What physically sets the maximum height of mountains on Earth — why ~8.8 km and not 20 km?
3. What are the actual measured rates of tectonic uplift (mm/yr) per setting, and how do they compare with erosion rates in the same places?
4. How does isostasy (crustal buoyancy) shape mountain belts — how deep are the roots, and what happens when erosion unloads them?
5. What is the supercontinent cycle, and what did Earth's terrain look like at its extremes (Rodinia, Pangaea — one landmass, one ocean)?
6. How do volcanoes build terrain — stratovolcanoes, shields, calderas — and what are their characteristic heights, slopes, and lifespans?
7. Why are there mountain chains running down the middle of oceans (mid-ocean ridges), and why are they rift valleys at their crests?
8. How do hotspots and mantle plumes build island chains (Hawaii), and why do the islands get older in one direction?
9. What did Earth's first continents look like, and how has terrain evolved over ~4 billion years (cratons, greenstone belts, the first mountains)?
10. What are real tectonic plate speeds and directions, and what do they sum to over millions of years (collision, subduction, accretion)?
11. Why do giant plateaus exist (Tibet, Altiplano, Colorado), and what holds them up?
12. What are normal faults, grabens, rift valleys (East Africa), and horsts — the extensional half of terrain building?
13. Why do subduction zones make arcs (curved island chains like the Aleutians and Japan)?
14. What happens at transform boundaries — what terrain do strike-slip faults leave behind?

## B. Erosion, rivers & the mathematical core of landscape evolution (Agent 2)

15. What is the stream power incision model and its full math (Ω = ρgQS; E = K·A^m·S^n; the channel concavity index m/n)?
16. What are Horton's laws and Hack's law — the quantitative anatomy of drainage networks — and how well do real rivers obey them?
17. How do rivers carve canyons — actual Grand Canyon incision rates and the total time it took?
18. Why do rivers meander, and what is the math linking meander wavelength to channel width? Why do cutoffs and oxbow lakes happen?
19. What sets a river's longitudinal profile, and what are knickpoints (waterfalls migrating upstream)?
20. How do hillslopes evolve — the diffusion equation for soil creep — and what sets the angle of stable bedrock vs soil-mantled slopes?
21. How do drainage divides migrate, and what is stream piracy? What decides which side wins?
22. How do deltas form, and why do they take different shapes (birdfoot Mississippi vs arcuate Nile)?
23. What are real erosion/denudation rates — by climate, rock type, relief, and tectonic setting (mm/kyr or t/km²/yr)?
24. What is the "memory" of a landscape — how long does it take to erase a mountain belt, and what survives as relict terrain?
25. What is base level (sea level, lake level, resistant rock), and what happens to a landscape when base level changes (terraces, entrenched meanders, drowned valleys)?
26. What is the fractal geometry of river networks and terrain profiles — measured fractal dimensions, and how do they vary by setting?
27. What is drainage density, what controls it (climate, rock, vegetation), and what range does it span?
28. What is the sediment cascade — where does eroded material go, and what are sediment yields per setting?
29. How do waterfalls actually retreat (knickpoint migration mechanics — plunge-pool undercutting vs uniform incision)?
30. What is the hypsometric integral of a watershed and what does it tell you about landscape age?

## C. Glacial, coastal & periglacial terrain (Agent 3)

31. How do glaciers carve U-valleys, cirques, arêtes, horns, and fjords — the "peaks of peaks" question — and at what measured rates?
32. What is the glacial buzzsaw, and why do glaciated ranges have suspiciously consistent peak elevations (the snowline equilibrium argument)?
33. What landforms does ice leave behind — moraines, drumlins, eskers, kames, outwash plains — with their characteristic sizes and spacing?
34. How many times was Earth glaciated, and how did the Quaternary ice ages (repeated ~100-kyr cycles) overprint today's terrain with relict features far from modern ice?
35. How do coasts erode — cliff retreat rates, wave-cut platforms, sea stacks, and arches?
36. How do beaches, spits, tombolos, and barrier islands form and migrate — the longshore-drift sediment budget?
37. What periglacial processes shape terrain — permafrost, solifluction lobes, patterned ground (stone circles/stripes), pingos, ice wedges?
38. How did glacial rebound (isostatic) and meltwater floods (Missoula/Channelled Scablands, Dry Falls) carve terrain in single catastrophic events?
39. Why is the highest point of many landscapes a glacial horn but the smoothest a plateau never touched by ice?

## D. Caves, karst & the underground (Agent 4)

40. How does limestone dissolve — the actual CO₂–carbonic-acid chemistry — and what sets the dissolution rate?
41. How do cave systems form (epigenic recharge vs hypogenic artesian), and what geometries result (branchwork vs maze vs spongework)?
42. What are measured rates of karst denudation and of cave passage enlargement — how long does a big trunk passage take?
43. What determines the vertical organization of caves — epikarst, vadose (air-filled) vs phreatic (water-filled) passages, water-table positions over time?
44. How do stalactites, stalagmites, columns, and flowstone grow — measured rates and what controls them (drip water Ca supersaturation)?
45. What are lava tubes — how do they form in flowing basalt, how big can they get, and what do they leave when drained and roof-collapsed?
46. What is cave breakdown — the mechanics of ceiling collapse — and what span widths are stable in what rock?
47. What are cenotes, sinkholes (cover-collapse vs solution), poljes, and tower karst — and what spatial patterns do they form across a landscape?
48. What is the climate inside caves (near-constant temperature, humidity, barometric airflow, CO₂ stratification)?
49. What other rock types host caves (gypsum, quartzite/silicate, glacier caves, talus caves, tafoni) and how do their formation mechanics differ?
50. How do caves interact with surface water — sinking streams, resurgences, underground drainage replacing surface drainage entirely in mature karst?

## E. Deserts, wind, sediment & climate geography (Agent 5)

51. What is the physics of wind-blown sand — Bagnold's saltation theory, shear velocity, impact threshold, and the mass-flux law (q ∝ u*³/g)?
52. What determines dune type — barchans, linear/seif, star, transverse, parabolic, dome — as a function of wind-direction variability and sand supply?
53. How fast do dunes and dune fields migrate, and how are entire sand seas (ergs) organized spatially?
54. What forms yardangs, zeugen, ventifacts, desert pavement, and deflation basins — erosion by wind alone, and its rate limits?
55. Why do deserts exist exactly where they do — Hadley-cell subsidence at ~30°, rain shadows, continentality, cold coasts — and how sharp are the boundaries?
56. How do loess deposits form and blanket whole regions downwind of deserts and glacial outwash?
57. What is desert varnish, caliche/calcrete (duricrusts), and desert soil in general — the slow chemical skin of arid terrain?
58. What are playas, salt flats, and ephemeral lakes, and how do they cycle (Racetrack Playa sliding rocks, Bonneville shorelines)?

## F. Vegetation spawn rates & spatial statistics (Agent 6)

59. What are actual measured tree densities (stems/ha) per biome and per successional stage — boreal, temperate, tropical, savanna, Mediterranean, mangrove?
60. What is the −3/2 (or −4/3) self-thinning law — the quantitative relation between stem density and tree size as a stand ages — and how well is it really obeyed?
61. How are plants spatially distributed — random, clumped, regular/overdispersed — and which spatial point processes (Poisson, Thomas, Neyman–Scott) actually fit field data?
62. What are measured seed dispersal distances and kernels per mode (wind, animal, ballistic, water), and what is the shape of the tail?
63. How does succession actually proceed after disturbance (fire, landslide, flood, lava, glacier retreat) — timelines, species turnover, and when does it reach a fluctuating "climax"?
64. What are densities of shrubs, ferns, grasses, forbs, and mosses per biome, and what ground-fraction do they cover?
65. What limits trees at treeline (temperature? growing season? wind?), at desert margins (moisture), and in swamps (oxygen)?
66. How does vegetation feed back on terrain itself — root wedging, bank stabilization, bioturbation, hillslope creep rates with vs without plants, fire-erosion coupling?
67. How clustered vs scattered are individual species within a biome (the "where exactly does a tree spawn" question — microsite preference, nurse plants, gap dynamics)?
68. What is known about global plant biomass/cover numbers (total forest area, tree count ~3.0 trillion, cover fractions) — and how were they measured?

## G. Procedural terrain generation synthesis (Agent 7)

69. How well does fBm/Perlin/simplex noise match real terrain statistics — power spectra, fractal dimensions, hypsometric curves — and exactly where does it fail (no drainage, no valleys, wrong scaling exponents)?
70. What are the standard procedural erosion algorithms — thermal erosion (talus angles), hydraulic droplet/particle erosion, grid-based fluvial LEMs, stream-power-uplift solvers — their costs, artifacts, and quality?
71. How do shipped games and tools generate terrain — World Machine, Gaea, Unreal Landscape, Houdini, No Man's Sky planet pipeline, Minecraft, Dwarf Fortress — what architecture does each use?
72. How do you generate hydrologically-correct terrain — depression filling (priority-flood), flow routing (D8/D-infinity), enforcing real drainage networks?
73. How do biome maps get generated procedurally — Whittaker diagrams, temperature/moisture/elevation fields, continentality, rain shadows from terrain itself?
74. What are domain warping, ridged noise, billowed noise, terracing, and each one's terrain type; what real landform is each trying to imitate?
75. How do you combine multiple scales correctly — when to multiply (billowy) vs add (fBm), octave count, lacunarity and gain values that match measured terrain spectra?
76. How do voxel engines specifically generate 3D terrain features that heightmaps can't — caves, overhangs, arches — with density fields, 3D noise carving, and cellular automata smoothing?
77. What is the pipeline for placing vegetation procedurally at real densities — biome-masked Poisson-disk/scattered placement, density maps from slope/moisture, and how shipped games do it?
78. What determines terrain LOD/streaming for near-planet-scale worlds (clipmaps, quadtree chunked LOD, NaN techniques) — briefly, as it constrains generation architecture?
79. How would you validate a generated terrain against real-Earth statistics (slope distributions, hypsometric curves, drainage density, valley cross-sections) — the acceptance tests?
80. What is the honest ceiling — which real terrain properties are computationally practical to capture in a real-time engine, and which should be faked, given everything Agents 1–6 found?
